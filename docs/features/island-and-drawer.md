# Island and drawer

The island is the small shape at the top centre of your screen. Yami lives there. It shows her state while you talk, and it opens a drawer of quick tools.

## What it does

- **Shows Yami's state.** She looks different while she listens, thinks and speaks.
- **Shows tool activity.** While Yami runs a tool, a small card under the island names the action, such as "Opening Spotify".
- **Shows now playing.** When media is playing, hover the small pill to see a card with progress and previous, play and next buttons.
- **Shows agent status.** With the AI workspace on, a pill shows the most urgent coding agent. See [AI workspace and agents](ai-and-agents.md).
- **Shows short messages.** Messages that do not fit the island, such as a missing key, appear in a caption at the bottom of the screen.
- **Tucks away.** After about eight quiet seconds, the island slims to a thin bar. Hover it, or start a voice turn, to bring Yami back.

## How to use it

1. **Open the drawer.** Click the island, or scroll down on it.
2. **Pick a tab.** The tabs are listed below.
3. **Close the drawer.** Scroll up while the drawer is at the top.
4. **Open the full app.** Double-click the island.
5. **Arm Yami's ability.** Right-click the island to arm an equipped ability, if you have one.

## The drawer tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Controls | Volume, brightness, dark or light mode, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Night Light, keep awake, colour picker, window layouts, workspaces, wallpaper tools and more. |
| Today | A clock, weather, the week, a focus timer, and your to-do list. |
| Stats | CPU, memory, GPU, disks, network speed, uptime and the busiest processes. |
| Clipboard | Recent copied text. Search it, pin items, copy them again or delete them. |
| Apps | Installed apps, favourites and recent apps. This tab also works as the launcher. |
| Wallpaper | Choose a folder of images and set one as your desktop wallpaper. |
| Power | Lock, sleep, restart or shut down. Each action asks you to confirm. |
| Radio | Listen to internet radio stations inside Yami. See [media and music](media-and-music.md). |
| AI | Agent activity, Open Yami Terminal, and your usage meters. Shown only when the AI workspace is on. |

## Settings that affect it

- **Settings > Behavior > Living flame.** Keeps the flame flickering while Yami is idle. It uses a little more power and is off by default.
- **Settings > Behavior > Reduced motion.** Stops most animations. Windows' own reduced-motion setting also works.
- **Settings > Behavior > Let Yami roam.** Shows the floating Yami on your desktop. See [dock and widget](dock-and-widget.md).
- **Settings > Behavior > Start quietly.** Starts with the island but without the main window.

## What it needs

- Nothing extra for the island and its basic tabs.
- Some controls depend on your hardware. For example, brightness needs a monitor that Windows can adjust, and the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth toggles depend on what Windows exposes.

## Privacy

Stats, the clipboard list, recent apps and your to-dos stay on this PC. Only the parts of your context you choose are sent to Gemini; see [providers and privacy](../providers-and-privacy.md).
