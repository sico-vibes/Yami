# Dock and desk widget

This page covers the app dock, hiding the Windows taskbar, the desk widget, and roaming Yami.

## The dock

The dock is a glass shelf along the bottom of your main screen. It holds your pinned apps first, then the apps that are open.

- **Open an app.** Click its icon. If the app is already open, its window comes forward.
- **Pin or remove an app.** Right-click an app and choose whether to keep it in the dock or remove it.
- **Activity dots.** A dot shows which apps have a window open.
- **Icons grow toward the pointer.** Turned off by Reduced motion.

Turn it on in **Settings > System > Dock**. It is off by default. The dock does not take focus and does not reserve screen space, so windows can sit under it.

### Hide the Windows taskbar

With the dock on, you can hide the Windows taskbar. Turn on **Hide the Windows taskbar** under the Dock setting. Yami turns on Windows' own auto-hide, so the taskbar appears when you move the pointer to the bottom edge. Turning the dock off puts the taskbar back, but only when Yami was the one that hid it.

## Desk widget

The desk widget is a small card on your desktop. It shows the time and date, today's weather (with the Weather module on), what is playing, and up to two active coding agents. If none are active, it says "All quiet."

1. Turn on **Desk widget** in **Settings > Modules**, or choose the Tinkerer or Everything preset.
2. Drag the card wherever you like. Yami remembers the spot and returns the card to the top-right corner if that screen is gone.

Choose a look in **Settings > System > Desk widget style**:

- **Card:** the full card described above.
- **Large clock:** the time, date and temperature, in large type.
- **Clock face:** an analogue clock face.

The widget sits below other windows, does not take focus, and does not appear on the taskbar. It updates the clock once a minute, the weather every half hour, and media only while something is playing. Nothing animates.

## Roaming Yami

Roaming Yami is a small Yami that floats around your desktop.

1. Turn on **Let Yami roam** in **Settings > Behavior**, or from the tray menu.
2. Yami dodges your cursor twice with a quip. Catch her to open Yami: click her to open the app, or drag her to move her.

She drifts to a new spot every few minutes. She skips this on battery or when a full-screen app is in front. She hides while a full-screen game, video or presentation is running. She naps after a minute of being left alone, and she flies into the island during voice and dictation.

Turn off roaming in the same place to remove her.

## Settings that affect it

- **Dock** and **Hide the Windows taskbar** (Settings > System).
- **Desk widget** module (Settings > Modules), and **Desk widget style** (Settings > System).
- **Let Yami roam** (Settings > Behavior), with **Reduced motion** to keep her still.

## What it needs

- Nothing extra for roaming Yami.
- The **Desk widget** module for the card.
- Windows' taskbar auto-hide, which Yami controls for you.

## Privacy

The dock reads the apps that have windows open on your PC. The widget shows the time, media and agent status from this PC. Weather is requested only when you turn it on. See [providers and privacy](../providers-and-privacy.md).
